# LICA — Figure captions

**Figure 1. Distribution of recorded essay-body lengths.** The histogram shows recorded word counts for the 100 essays in 25-word bins. The dashed vertical line marks 250 words, a descriptive threshold rather than an exclusion criterion.

**Figure 2. Agreement between the two historical human raters by criterion.** Points show quadratic-weighted Cohen’s kappa; horizontal bars show pointwise 95% percentile confidence intervals from 5,000 paired essay-level bootstrap replicates. Sample sizes reflect the exclusion of unresolved rating conflicts for each criterion.

**Figure 3. Bland–Altman plots for the two historical human raters.** Each panel plots Human1 minus Human2 against the mean of their paired scores. Marker area is proportional to the number of pairs at the same coordinates. Solid lines indicate mean signed differences; dashed lines indicate descriptive limits of agreement (mean difference ± 1.96 sample standard deviations). The limits should not be interpreted as guaranteed 95% coverage for these bounded ordinal scores.

**Figure 4. Distribution of Qwen2.5-7B ratings by criterion.** Bars show the number of essays assigned each score on the 1–5 scale (100 essays per criterion). Across all four criteria, 399 of 400 ratings were at levels 3 or 4.

**Figure 5. Human–human and model–human agreement on matched essays.** Within each criterion, all three pairs were evaluated on the same essays. Points show quadratic-weighted Cohen’s kappa; horizontal bars show pointwise 95% percentile confidence intervals from 5,000 paired essay-level bootstrap replicates, without adjustment for multiple comparisons. The dotted line marks zero kappa. Model–human comparisons are exploratory because equivalence between the historical human scoring instrument and the model’s draft rubric was not established.
